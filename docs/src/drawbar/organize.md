# Your library

Your library holds the sounds, songs, samples and notes you keep in drawbar. It
appears as **This computer** in the sidebar. On the desktop it is a folder of
ordinary files, so you can back it up, sync it, or use a folder you already have
instead. In a browser it lives in the browser's storage unless you open a folder.

## Add files

1. Drop files on the window, or choose **File ▸ Import…**. drawbar copies the
   files it supports into **This computer**. Your originals stay where they were.
2. To group sounds, for example for a gig, choose **New ▸ New folder** and name
   it, such as *Friday set*. Drag sounds into it.
3. For stage cues, choose **New ▸ Text note**, type, and choose **File ▸ Save**.
   Move the note into the folder with its sounds.

Already have your files arranged in a folder?
[Open that folder as your library](folders.md#opening-another-folder) and work on
them where they are. **New** also offers blank sounds by instrument family. Some
start with every control at zero.

## Tag sounds

Tags let you find sounds again by band, venue or song, wherever they are filed.

1. Select the rows. Command-click (Ctrl-click on Windows or Linux) adds a row,
   and Shift-click adds a range.
2. Choose **Tag** in the row's right-click menu, or **Add tag** in the inspector.
3. Pick a tag, or choose **New tag…** and type a name, such as *Encore*.

Notes and WAVs take tags too. In the inspector's **Tags** card, click a tag to
apply or remove it across the selection. Rename a tag to another tag's name to
merge the two. Removing a tag leaves its sounds in place.

## Find a sound

Click the search box at the top, or press Command-K (Ctrl-K), and type part of a
name. In **Library**, click a column heading to sort. The **Modified** column
says when a file on this computer was last changed. Choose a kind or tag on the
left to narrow the list. Two tags show the sounds that have both. Click a
selected filter again to remove it.

With an instrument connected, more columns help:

| Column | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Where** | **computer**, **keyboard** or **both**. **both =** means the copies match, and **both ≠** that they differ. |
| **Needs** | The sample or piano a program uses, when drawbar knows it. The inspector lists it under **Dependencies**. |

Choose **Waiting to send** to find sounds queued for the instrument.

## Rename, remove or undo

Select a file and press F2 to rename it. Removing a folder moves its contents up
one level. If drawbar finds hidden files in it, **Show them** lets you look
before you decide.

**Edit ▸ Undo** names the last library change it can take back, and **Edit ▸
Redo** repeats it. A move, save, deletion or tag change across several files is
one step. Undo lasts while this window has the library open. To drop an edit you
haven't saved, choose **Revert** in the open document.

Deleted and overwritten files are kept for Undo up to 1 GiB, oldest first out.
Quitting empties that trash.

## Export files for someone else

Choose **Export…** in an open document to save a separate copy. To export
several, select their rows under **This computer** and choose **File ▸ Export…**.
For a song or program that needs other sounds to play,
[export a bundle](bundles.md).
