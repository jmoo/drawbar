# Build your own sounds

drawbar turns your own WAV recordings into sounds the keyboard plays. Pick the
route that matches the recordings you have.

<div class="cards">
<div class="card">

**One WAV per note**

A sound spread across zones of the keyboard, such as a marimba recorded at a
few pitches.

[Make a sample instrument](samples.md)

</div>
<div class="card">

**One WAV per stroke**

A piano with velocity layers, pedal resonance or release sounds.

[Build a piano](pianos.md#a-new-library)

</div>
<div class="card">

**One long WAV of notes**

Notes played one after another in a single take. drawbar finds each note.

[Make a piano from a recording](recording.md)

</div>
</div>

A piano that is too large for the keyboard's free memory can be
[trimmed to fit](pianos.md#trim-to-fit).
